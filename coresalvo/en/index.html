Aim, fire, and bring
the diorama down.

Core Salvo is a brick breaker set in tiny 3D dioramas. Every shot comes from the "core" in the middle of the board. Your balls bounce around and fly back to the core. A Zen rock garden, a five-story pagoda, cherry blossoms at night, a space station: break every orb on the board to clear each of the 50 stages. No sign-up, no in-app purchases. Just open it and play.

Android / Free (ads, no in-app purchases)

How to play

  1. 1

    Aim with the stick, then FIRE

    Touch the screen and an aiming stick grows from your finger. Aim with its tip and press FIRE. To roll the ball, aim close to the core. The farther you aim, the higher it flies.

  2. 2

    Balls come home

    Your balls bounce around the diorama and come back to the core. Hold the core to recall every ball at once.

  3. 3

    Break every orb

    Break every orb on the board within the turn limit to clear the stage. The fewer turns you use, the closer you are to a star.

Features

50 dioramas

A Zen rock garden, a paper-screen room, cherry blossoms at night, a five-story pagoda, a shipwreck, a space station and more. Five worlds of ten hand-made stages.

Aim into the air

Blocks sit on the floor and float in the air above it. Hold still for a moment to see through the blocks in the air and aim behind them.

Tricks in every stage

Leap high walls off jump pads, send balls through glass pipes, cut cords to drop what hangs, and knock out the base to bring towers down. Each world brings new tricks.

Three stars

One star each for the turns, the percentage of blocks broken, and the stage's own task, like "Send a ball through a pipe into an orb".

Any angle you like

Rotate, pan and pinch to zoom the board. Tilt your phone to look up and down (can be turned off in the settings).

Hidden +1 BALL blocks

Each world hides a +1 BALL block. Find it to fire one more ball. Cleared stages can be played again any time.

Calm to play

Work out how to bring each diorama down, and enjoy the moment it falls just as you planned, at your own pace.

Balls come back

Every ball bounces back to the core. You never lose a ball.

Try again any time

Out of turns? Retry the stage right away and try a new plan.

A few minutes a stage

One stage on a short break, a whole world on a quiet afternoon.

No flashing

No full-screen flashing effects, so it stays easy on the eyes.

Easy to pick up

  • Aim with the stick: the tip of the stick from your finger is where you aim. Then just press FIRE.
  • Star goals up front: each stage shows its star goals before you start. Tap the score board at the top to check them while you play.
  • How to play on the title: tap "?" on the title screen to read about the controls and tricks any time.
  • Auto-save: pick up where you left off with CONTINUE.
  • English or Japanese: follows your phone's language.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, you can play it all for free. Ads are shown, but there are no in-app purchases and no sign-up.

The next world won't open.

The next world opens when you have 23 stars from the world before it. Each star comes from the turns, the blocks broken or the task, so tap the score board at the top to see which goals you still need.

My balls keep flying up.

The farther you aim, the higher the ball flies. To roll it along the floor, aim close to the core.

I ran out of turns.

Tap RETRY to play the stage again right away. Jump pads, pipes and other tricks can often break a lot at once.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, your records are saved automatically, and only on your device.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It is Android only for now.
